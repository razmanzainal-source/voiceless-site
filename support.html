Common questions

Speech recognition does not start

Conversation Mode asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission when you tap Tap to listen. Allow both permissions in device Settings. On-device speech models vary by device and language; choose a language that is installed on the device. You can always type the message manually.

Translation is unavailable

Translation supports English and Bahasa Melayu only. Turn it on in Conversation Mode or Settings, then download the language models. Model downloads require an internet connection. If the download fails, check the connection and retry. Downloaded models can be removed in Settings.

Speech sounds robotic or is missing

VOICELESS uses voices installed by the device vendor. Install a higher-quality voice in device settings and select it in VOICELESS Settings. If speech is unavailable, messages can still be displayed as large text.

Backups

Backups contain personal vocabulary, images, audio, settings and optional emergency information. Backup archives are not encrypted. Store exported backups securely and keep a copy somewhere safe.

Privacy

Read the VOICELESS Privacy Policy. Core communication works offline. Optional translation model downloads use the internet and Google ML Kit.

Emergency disclaimer

VOICELESS is a communication aid. It does not contact emergency services, share location or monitor the user. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or a trusted person directly.

Soalan lazim

Pengecaman pertuturan tidak bermula

Mod Perbualan meminta kebenaran mikrofon dan pengecaman pertuturan apabila anda menekan Ketik untuk mendengar. Benarkan kedua-dua kebenaran dalam Tetapan peranti. Model pertuturan pada peranti berbeza mengikut peranti dan bahasa; pilih bahasa yang dipasang pada peranti. Anda sentiasa boleh menaip mesej secara manual.

Terjemahan tidak tersedia

Terjemahan menyokong bahasa Inggeris dan Bahasa Melayu sahaja. Aktifkannya dalam Mod Perbualan atau Tetapan, kemudian muat turun model bahasa. Muat turun model memerlukan sambungan internet. Jika muat turun gagal, semak sambungan dan cuba lagi. Model yang dimuat turun boleh dibuang dalam Tetapan.

Suara kedengaran robotik atau tiada

VOICELESS menggunakan suara yang dipasang oleh vendor peranti. Pasang suara yang lebih baik dalam tetapan peranti dan pilih dalam Tetapan VOICELESS. Jika suara tidak tersedia, mesej masih boleh dipaparkan sebagai teks besar.

Sandaran mengandungi kosa kata peribadi, gambar, audio, tetapan dan maklumat kecemasan pilihan. Arkib sandaran tidak disulitkan. Simpan sandaran eksport dengan selamat dan simpan salinan di tempat yang selamat.
